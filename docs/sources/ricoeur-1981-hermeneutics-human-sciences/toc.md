## Operational map for the Mode 2 (Hermeneutic) critic skill

Per Report 2 and the manifest, the operative chapters for Mode 2 are **Chs. 4, 5, and 8**:

- **Ch. 4 ("The hermeneutical function of distanciation", p. 93)** — the distanciation/appropriation pair as the mediating structure of interpretation; pairs with Ch. 7 (Appropriation, p. 144) for the full treatment.
- **Ch. 5 ("What is a text? Explanation and understanding", p. 107)** — the explanation/understanding dialectic as Ricoeur's correction to a Gadamer-only hermeneutics; the most directly extractable methodological essay in the volume.
- **Ch. 8 ("The model of the text: meaningful action considered as a text", p. 159)** — the text-analogy applied to action; supplies a hermeneutic vocabulary for interpreting non-textual material.

For dialogical-move extraction, **Chs. 4 + 5 + 8** are the canonical trio. Ch. 7 (Appropriation) pairs naturally with Ch. 4 if more material on the receiving side of interpretation is wanted.

## Notes on positioning relative to Gadamer

Ricoeur's hermeneutics in this volume engages Gadamer (whose *Truth and Method* is the parallel canonical primary source for Mode 2) on multiple points. Ch. 2 ("Hermeneutics and the critique of ideology") engages the Habermas-Gadamer debate from a position that mediates between the two. Ch. 3 ("Phenomenology and hermeneutics") situates Ricoeur's project against Husserl-Heidegger phenomenology.

For a Mode 2 skill that wants the explanation/understanding dialectic that Gadamer downplays, Ricoeur is essential reading alongside Gadamer's *Truth and Method* Part II. The two together cover the full hermeneutic apparatus the Mode 2 skill needs.
