## Translator's Introduction (excerpt — what the Reeve edition is doing)

Reeve's notes describe themselves as "a place to start, not a place to finish — a first step in the vast" project of reading the *Metaphysics*. The translation aims at philosophical accuracy without smoothing the disjointed-treatise character of Aristotle's text. Reeve preserves both Bekker pagination (e.g., 982a1) and traditional book-chapter divisions, with cross-references to corresponding sections of *Physics*, *Categories*, *Posterior Analytics*, etc.

The translator's framing of Beta in particular (from his book-level note): Beta is "Aristotle's set of fourteen *aporiai*" — programmatically positioned after Alpha (the historical-doxographic survey) and before Gamma (the constructive science of being *qua* being). The puzzles are not merely cleared away by Gamma onward; many are re-engaged across Books Z, H, Θ, M, N, with Beta's enumeration providing the through-line.

## Operational map for the exploratory-default deliverable

Per the manifest, **Book Beta (III)** is the operational target — Aristotle's *aporiai* are the canonical Western statement of philosophical inquiry as a movement *through* puzzles (*dia-poreuesthai*) toward articulated impasse (*aporia*) and out (*euporia*). For an exploratory-default disposition, the sequence Beta enacts — enumerating puzzles, holding them open without forced resolution, working through one before turning to the next — is a procedural template for sustained inquiry that does not collapse into either premature thesis or aimless wandering.

For directive extraction:

- **Β 1 (the enumeration of P1–14)** — the methodological move: stating puzzles in pairs, with both horns of each, before any attempt at resolution. This is the most directly transferable chapter for procedural directives ("when faced with a contested question, enumerate its puzzles before adjudicating; state the strongest formulation of each side").
- **Β 2–6 (the discussions of individual puzzles)** — worked examples of how Aristotle works through an *aporia*: locating the assumption that generates the puzzle, distinguishing senses, pressing the consequence of each side. Useful for grounding the "rigorous exploration" disposition in a concrete worked tradition.
