## Each chapter follows a uniform structure

Per the Preface, each chapter contains:
- "In a Nutshell" overview
- Key Terms Explained
- Introduction
- Substantive content (the protocol guidance for that stage)
- Toolbox (concrete tools/resources)
- Summary + Key Learning Points
- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- Suggestions for Further Information

This regular structure makes chunking and per-chapter directive extraction reliable: the substantive directives are concentrated in the body sections; the FAQs translate those directives into surface-verifiable triggers.

## Operational map for the research-procedure deliverable

Per the manifest target ("search-strategy and SPIDER chapters"), the most operative chapters are:

- **Ch. 4 ("Defining Your Scope", p. 93)** — frameworks for scope (PICO, SPIDER, etc.); review protocols (p. 116). Direct match for the procedure document's question-formulation stage.
- **Ch. 5 ("Searching the Literature", p. 124)** — eight-stage search process (pp. 126–146); how to translate review questions into search strategies. Direct match for the procedure document's source-acquisition stage.
- **Ch. 6 ("Assessing the Evidence Base", p. 158)** — quality assessment, validity/reliability/applicability, hierarchies of evidence. Useful for the procedure's source-quality directives.
- **Ch. 8 ("Synthesising and Analysing Qualitative Studies", p. 235)** — thematic synthesis, framework synthesis, meta-ethnography (p. 250). Relevant to the philosophical-synthesis component of the procedure (with the caveat that meta-ethnography needs adaptation per Report 1).
- **Ch. 10 ("Writing Up, Presenting and Disseminating", p. 309)** — PRISMA 2020 reporting standards, including PRISMA-ScR (the scoping-review extension at p. 332). The reporting-standard discussion is what the procedure document should imitate structurally.

For Tier 1 use focused on building the procedure document, **Ch. 4 + Ch. 5 + Ch. 10's PRISMA-ScR section** are the operational core. Ch. 1 (review-family taxonomy) is useful early-orientation reading; the synthesis chapters (7, 8, 9) are background unless the procedure document is specifically positioning itself within thematic-synthesis or meta-ethnographic methodology.
