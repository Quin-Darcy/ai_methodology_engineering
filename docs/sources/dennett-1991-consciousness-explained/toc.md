## Preface (excerpt — what the book is)

Dennett describes the book as the consolidation of "thirty years of thinking, talking, and writing" about the mind-body problem, with the writing itself begun January 1990 and finished a year later (p. xi). The argument is presented as an "odyssey through many fields" rather than solitary cogitation, with solutions "inextricably woven into a fabric of dialogue and disagreement, where we often learn more from bold mistakes than from cautious equivocation."

## Operational map for the Mode 4 critic skill (Dennettian stress-testing)

Per Report 2, *Consciousness Explained* is "the highest-value single source" for Mode 4 dialogical-move extraction because it shows Dennett doing the moves rather than describing them. The most operative chapters:

- **Ch. 10 ("Show and Tell", p. 285)** — "Zombies, Zimboes, and the User Illusion" (sub-section 4); "Problems with Folk Psychology" (sub-section 5)
- **Ch. 11 ("Dismantling the Witness Protection Program", p. 321)** — Blindsight as "partial zombiehood"; "Filling In" versus Finding Out; the Otto dialogue
- **Ch. 12 ("Qualia Disqualified", p. 369)** — Inverted qualia thought-experiment dismantled; "Epiphenomenal" qualia; the Mary-the-color-scientist treatment
- **Ch. 14 ("Consciousness Imagined", p. 431)** — The Chinese Room, Nagel's bat, and the conscious-robot thought experiment

These chapters are the worked-example library that *Intuition Pumps* (2013) summarizes. For chunking and extraction, treat each as a unit; the sub-sections are the natural finer-grained boundaries if needed.

For methodological orientation: **Ch. 4 ("A Method for Phenomenology")** introduces heterophenomenology as Dennett's answer to first-person reports, and is the closest the book comes to a methodological primer.
