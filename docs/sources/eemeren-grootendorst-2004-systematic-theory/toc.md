## Operational map for the Mode 1 (Pragma-Dialectical) critic skill

Per Report 2 and the manifest, the operative chapters for Mode 1 are:

- **Ch. 3 (Model of a critical discussion, p. 42)** — the four-stage dialectical model.
- **Ch. 6 (Rules for a critical discussion, p. 123)** — the canonical ten-rule list, the operational core of the framework.
- **Ch. 7 (Fallacies, p. 158)** — fallacies-as-rule-violations, paired 1:1 with Ch. 6's rules.

For an authoring pass on the Mode 1 skill, the most directly extractable material is **the ten rules in Ch. 6 plus their paired fallacy treatment in Ch. 7**. Ch. 8's "ten commandments" condense the rules into a learner-friendly form that reads almost as candidate directives without rephrasing.

For complementary worked-example material with extended dialogues and exercises, see `eemeren-grootendorst-snoeck-henkemans-2002-argumentation-aep` (the textbook companion by the same Amsterdam group).
