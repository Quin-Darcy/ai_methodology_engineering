## Notes on a few entries

- **Williamson is two different books across reports.** *Doing Philosophy* (2018) / *Philosophical Method: A VSI* (2020) is the research-procedure source. *The Philosophy of Philosophy* (Blackwell 2007) is the Mode 4 critique source. Both are needed for different purposes.
- **Skinner's *Visions of Politics* Vol. 1 is the consolidated reference.** The 1969 *History and Theory* essay is the historically operative document and is still more frequently cited; acquire both.
- **Pragma-dialectics has updated since 2004.** Van Eemeren's *Argumentation Theory: A Pragma-Dialectical Perspective* (Springer 2018) adds strategic maneuvering. Useful but not essential for a v1 Mode 1 skill.
- **Walton's *Argumentation Schemes* is a reference book, not a textbook.** For step-by-step worked applications use *Methods of Argumentation* (2013) or *Fundamentals of Critical Argumentation* (2006).
- **Worked-example asymmetry across modes.** Modes 1 and 4 have well-developed textbook literatures; Modes 2 and 3 do not. For Mode 2, use Weinsheimer's commentary plus Palmer's "Thirty Theses". For Mode 3, the dialogical pairing in Tully's *Meaning and Context* is the closest substitute.
